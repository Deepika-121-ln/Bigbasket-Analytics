# Bigbasket Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview 
The **BigBasket Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze product performance, sales trends, customer ratings, and category-wise insights. The dashboard helps businesses understand product distribution, pricing strategies, and brand performance through interactive visualizations.

## Objectives
- Analyze product distribution across different categories and sub-categories.
- Monitor average sales price and customer ratings.
- Compare category-wise sales and market prices.
- Identify the top-performing brands and product categories.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

  ## Dataset

The dataset contains product-related information, including:

- Product Name
- Category
- Sub Category
- Brand
- Product Type
- Sale Price
- Market Price
- Rating

  ## Dashboard Features

- Displays key KPIs including Total Products, Total Categories, Total Brands, Average Rating, and Average Sales Price.
- Analyzes product distribution by category, sub-category, and customer ratings.
- Compares average sale price and market price across different product categories.
- Identifies the Top 10 brands based on product count and Top 5 product categories.
- Provides interactive filters for Category, Sub Category, Brand, and Product Type.
